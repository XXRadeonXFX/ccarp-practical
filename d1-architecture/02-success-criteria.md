# Success Criteria
-> Out of 10 incidents, correctly identify the type/category of at least 8.
-> Out of 10 incidents, suggest the right troubleshooting guide (runbook) for at least 8.
-> Whenever you recommend a runbook, show the part/evidence from that runbook that supports your recommendation.
-> If there is no suitable runbook, don't make one up. Say no matching runbook found.
-> If you're not sure about the answer, set Escalation = Yes so a human can review it.
-> Every answer must contain all the required fields. Don't leave any required field missing.
-> The system should give its answer within 10 seconds when tested locally.
